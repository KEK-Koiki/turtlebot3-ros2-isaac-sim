# Project provenance and attribution

This showcase presents selected implementation and evidence from Elijah Koiki’s RAS 545 Lab 3 submission. It covers Parts A–F of [TurtleBot 3 ROS2 Integration, Sensors, Localization, and Navigation](https://tatwik19.github.io/labs/lab-03-isaac-sim/).

## Course and asset credits

The lab page credits instructor Prof. Sangram Redkar and authors Sai Srinivas Tatwik Meesala, Rajesh S Aouti, and Prajval Arora. The supplied TurtleBot starter included the robot geometry, joints, collision geometry, and rigid-body physics. The project’s focus is ROS 2 integration and validation of that supplied model.

Raw robot and room assets, the course starter, packaged USD checkpoints, the full course report, and the complete submission archive are excluded from this public repository. Course and NVIDIA content retain their respective ownership and terms. Screenshots and the demo show the supplied models as used in the simulation; they do not assert original authorship of those models.

## Contributions and automated assistance

The original report discloses that automated assistance operated the local simulation, repaired graph scripts, collected evidence, and assembled the report. This showcase preserves that disclosure and does not assert that all implementation or debugging was independently authored by the student. Portfolio documentation and the saved-evidence checker were also prepared with automated assistance.

The selected original source files and logs are copied unchanged from the supplied archive. The new saved-evidence checker evaluates consistency of those saved records. Portfolio preparation did not repeat the GPU simulation.

## Demonstrated scope

Parts A–F cover physics inspection, simulation time, differential drive, lidar, camera data, odometry, and TF. AMCL, mapping, and Nav2 goal execution are outside the demonstrated work. The original submission records instructor check-off and acceptance of the Jazzy environment difference as pending.
